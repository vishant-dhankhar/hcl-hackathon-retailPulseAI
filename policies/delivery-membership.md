# Delivery and membership policy

RetailPulse AI hackathon demonstration policy. Version: demo-2. Effective from: 10 October 2026. Country: India. Applies to all product categories and membership tiers. These are fictional demonstration rules, not a promise by a real retailer.

## Standard delivery charges

Standard members pay INR 99 for standard delivery. Silver members pay INR 99 when the merchandise subtotal is below INR 1,999 and receive free standard delivery when the subtotal is INR 1,999 or more. Gold and Platinum members receive free standard delivery regardless of merchandise subtotal. The threshold uses the merchandise subtotal before delivery charges.

## Express delivery charges

Express delivery costs INR 199 for Standard and Silver members, INR 99 for Gold members, and INR 0 for Platinum members. Express delivery is offered only when the selected store supports it. Express charges replace standard delivery charges; the two charges are not added together. The MVP does not promise a delivery time or delivery date.

## Store pickup

Store pickup is free for Standard, Silver, Gold and Platinum members. Customers select a physical store with sufficient stock for every item. Customers should collect an order only after its status becomes READY_FOR_PICKUP. The assigned store manager marks the order COLLECTED after collection.

## Stock and fulfillment

The catalogue displays the assortment and available quantity for the selected store. Stock at another location does not guarantee stock at the selected store. Delivery orders use the central fulfillment warehouse; pickup orders use the selected physical store. Availability is checked again when an order is placed. Adding an item to the shopping bag does not reserve stock. Customers must reduce quantities or choose another available fulfillment option if checkout reports insufficient stock.

## Membership and payment

Membership tiers affect delivery charges, support priority and applicable policy access. Tiers do not grant staff or administrator permissions. Membership is maintained by an administrator. Checkout is simulated for this hackathon MVP: no real payment is collected and no payment gateway is connected.
