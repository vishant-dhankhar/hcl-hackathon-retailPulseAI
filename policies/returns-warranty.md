# Returns and warranty policy

RetailPulse AI hackathon demonstration policy. Version: demo-2. Effective from: 10 October 2026. Country: India. Applies to all membership tiers. These are fictional demonstration rules for support answers. Return requests and warranty claims are reviewed by staff; the MVP does not automate returns, refunds or warranty approval.

## Opened mobile phones

Opened mobile phones are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. A suspected manufacturing defect in a mobile phone can be reported through support within seven days of delivery or collection for inspection. Reporting a defect does not automatically approve a return or replacement. Provide the order number and a description of the issue; staff will explain the inspection process.

## Discounted purchases

Discounted products follow their category-specific return rules. A discount does not create a separate return entitlement. Proof of purchase is required for a store return request. If no applicable category rule is present in the approved policies, support must ask an administrator to confirm eligibility rather than invent a return window or approval.

## Refrigerator warranty

Haven refrigerators include a one-year manufacturer warranty for this demonstration. Damage caused by misuse is excluded. Customers should raise a support ticket linked to the purchase order and describe the fault. Staff review the claim and explain the next steps; the application does not automatically decide warranty eligibility or issue a replacement.

## Other products and missing evidence

This policy does not specify a return window or warranty duration for headphones, speakers, computers, wearables or other home appliances. A customer asking about an unspecified product should be directed to support for confirmation. Staff and the assistant must not apply the mobile-phone inspection period or refrigerator warranty to another category without an approved policy covering that category.

## Refunds

The hackathon checkout does not collect money, so cancellation or a support resolution does not trigger a real refund. No refund amount or processing deadline is promised by this demonstration policy.
